Pre-release components now show their lifecycle state in the editor.
`ActivityIndicator`, `ChatMessage`, `ChatMessageList`, `FileTrigger`, `ListBox`
and `ModalPage` (Experimental), and `DefaultPage`, `DropZone`, `FormActionBar`,
`Item`, `ItemGroup`, `Markdown`, `Popover`, `PublicPageLayout`, `RangeSlider`,
`Region`, `Slider`, `Splitter`, `Steps` and `Tree` (Beta) carry the
`@experimental` or `@beta` JSDoc tag in their type definitions.
